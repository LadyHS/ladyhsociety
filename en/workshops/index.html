Cocreate or participate in our workshops

  1. Participants

    The workshops are for groups, we have tested them with Middle and High Schoolers, we've designed them to be as inclusive as possible.

  2. Objectives

    Enjoy a fun and creative moment, understand our society, its environmental and social issues, foster critical mind and respectful dialogue, improve or consolidate self-confidence

  1. Content

    Session 1 (3h): understand the themes with a serious game and a giant puzzle. Sessions 2 (3x3h) : with theatre professionnals, enter Lady H's year 3000. Sessions 3 and/or 4 (3x3h or 6x3h): we create illustrations, sculptures, sound effects and soundtracks with artists to build year 3000

  2. Partners

    We build the workshops with artists and the Ligue of Teaching (French network), we were funded by the DRAC Nouvelle Aquitaine in 2024
